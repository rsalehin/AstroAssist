# ADR-0014: BSD-3-Clause license, JOSS-ready repository

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-09-21 · **Deciders:** Abir (maintainer)

## Context
Astronomy community norm (Astropy, astroquery) is BSD; potential JOSS/RNAAS paper.

## Decision
BSD-3-Clause for core; CITATION.cff, codemeta.json, CONTRIBUTING, CODE_OF_CONDUCT from day one.

## Alternatives considered
MIT (equivalent, less common in astro); Apache-2 (patent clause, fine but less conventional); GPL (blocks some institutional use).

## Consequences
Third-party GPL components kept separable.
